# MarketMind – AI Business Research & Market Intelligence Agent

MarketMind is an Agentic AI system designed to automate business research and market intelligence.

Instead of simply answering a business question, MarketMind follows a multi-step research workflow:

**Understand → Plan → Research → Analyze → Report**

The system can break a broad business question into smaller research tasks, use tools and knowledge retrieval to gather relevant information, analyze the collected findings, maintain research context and state, and generate a structured market intelligence report.

---

## Problem Statement

Business research often requires collecting information from multiple sources.

Comparing competitors, identifying trends, and organizing findings can be time-consuming. Users may also receive information without a clear research plan or structured final report.

MarketMind addresses this problem by providing an AI-based research workflow that plans research tasks, uses tools and knowledge retrieval, analyzes findings, and produces structured market intelligence.

---

## Proposed Solution

MarketMind follows five major stages:

1. **Understand** – Interprets the user's business research objective.
2. **Plan** – Breaks the objective into smaller research tasks.
3. **Research** – Uses tools and knowledge retrieval to gather relevant information.
4. **Analyze** – Connects findings, compares information, and identifies insights.
5. **Report** – Produces a structured and readable market intelligence report.
